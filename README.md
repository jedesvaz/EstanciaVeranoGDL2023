#  Summer Research Internship — GDL 2023

Repository of files and scripts produced during the research internship at the **Instituto Jalisciense de Cancerología (IJC)**, in Guadalajara, Jalisco, México, during the summer of 2023.

---

##  Research Objective

Investigate the **relationship between Vitamin D levels and cancer** in oncological patients at the IJC, through statistical analysis of clinical data collected at the institute.

---

---

##  Statistical Analyses Performed

- **Descriptive statistics** — frequency distributions and summary measures
- **Normality tests** — to validate assumptions before inferential analysis
- **Spearman correlation** — non-parametric association between Vitamin D levels and clinical variables
- **Logistic regression** — predictive modeling of oncological outcomes based on Vitamin D

---

##  Tools Used

- **SPSS** — primary statistical analysis and output (`.sav`, `.spv` files)
- **Python / Jupyter Notebook** — supplementary analysis and visualization
- **Microsoft Excel** — data set allocation

---

##  Study Population

Oncological patients attended at the **Instituto Jalisciense de Cancerología (IJC)**, Guadalajara, Jalisco, México.
